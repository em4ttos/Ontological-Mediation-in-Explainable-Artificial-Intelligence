# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This repository supports research on ontological mediation, concept-level representation, explainable artificial intelligence, formalization, and related computational artifacts. Critical discussion is welcome and may be technically or philosophically demanding, but it must remain professional and directed at ideas, evidence, methods, models, and implementations.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- engage respectfully and in good faith;
- provide criticism that is specific, evidence-based, and relevant to the work;
- distinguish disagreement with an argument from judgment about a person;
- acknowledge uncertainty, provenance, and relevant prior work where appropriate;
- avoid harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, intimidation, or deliberate disruption;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual-property constraints, and security-sensitive information.

## Research disagreement

Strong disagreement is compatible with this repository. Adversarial review, counterexamples, requests for clarification, and challenges to novelty or formal correctness are welcome when they are expressed constructively and with sufficient technical context.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, repository-linked communication, and other interactions carried out in the name of this project.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer may edit, hide, reject, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.

If a matter should not be raised publicly, contact the repository maintainer through the maintainer's GitHub profile before sharing sensitive details.

## Attribution

This policy is an adapted project-specific code of conduct intended for an academic and technical research repository.
